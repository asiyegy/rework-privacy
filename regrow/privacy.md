# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Densio app ("Application") for mobile devices, created by Asiye ("Service Provider") as a freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

## Summary

Densio is a documentation and measurement tool for tracking hair-transplant and hair-treatment progress. To generate measurements, the scalp photos you capture are sent to third-party AI service providers (Google) for automated analysis. Photos and measurement history are stored only on your device. Densio does not perform face recognition and does not create or store any faceprint, face template, or face geometry data (see "Face Data" below). This policy explains what is collected, how, why, and with whom it is shared.

## Information the Application Collects, and How

1. Capture data — collected only when you actively take a measurement:

*   Photos of your scalp regions that you choose to capture, sent for AI analysis at the moment you request a measurement. When you photograph a frontal region (hairline or temples), part of your forehead or face may incidentally appear in the frame; such images are handled exactly like every other captured photo and are described further under "Face Data" below
*   AI-generated measurement scores (density, coverage, hairline position) derived from those photos
*   Optional treatment information you enter (for example, a treatment start date)

2. Automatically collected data:

*   Device information (model, operating system version, language) and IP address
*   Usage data (screens visited, features used, session duration, time and date)
*   Crash and diagnostic data
*   Advertising-related identifiers, only if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission

3. Purchase data: subscription status and transaction identifiers. Card details never reach the Service Provider.

## How the Information Is Used

*   To generate measurements: captured photos are processed by AI services solely to produce density, coverage, and hairline scores; the optional AI before/after composite feature sends the two photos you select solely to produce that composite image
*   To operate, maintain, and improve the Application (analytics, crash diagnostics)
*   To manage subscriptions and the free-trial entitlement
*   To send optional measurement reminders you schedule
*   To measure advertising effectiveness (only with your App Tracking Transparency consent)
*   The Service Provider may use contact information you provide to send important notices

## Third Parties That Process Data

The Application shares data with the following processors, strictly to provide the service. Each provider is bound by data-protection terms that provide protection equal to or greater than this policy:

*   [Google LLC](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) — Gemini AI models via Firebase AI Logic process captured photos to generate measurement scores and optional composite images. Google also provides Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, and Cloud Messaging for app operation.
*   [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) — subscription management
*   Apple — payment processing through the App Store
*   [Meta Platforms](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy) — advertising measurement, only with App Tracking Transparency consent

The Service Provider may also disclose information as required by law (for example, to comply with a subpoena), to protect rights or safety, to investigate fraud, or to respond to a government request.

## Face Data

Densio is a hair-measurement tool. It does not use face recognition, face identification, face tracking, or any other biometric technology, and it does not knowingly collect face data for any purpose.

**What is not collected.** The Application does not use the TrueDepth camera, ARKit face tracking, depth or face-mesh capture, or any face-detection or face-recognition framework. It never creates, derives, stores, or transmits a faceprint, face template, face signature, facial geometry, facial landmark map, or any other biometric identifier. It never uses an image to identify or authenticate a person, never matches an image against any face database, and never infers age, gender, ethnicity, emotion, or identity from an image.

**Incidental capture.** Because frontal scalp regions (hairline and temples) sit directly above the face, part of your forehead — and in some framings part of your face — may incidentally appear in a photo you capture, including when you use the front-facing camera to frame the shot. Densio uses only the standard wide-angle camera, crops each capture to the selected scalp region before analysis, and analyzes only hair characteristics (density, coverage, and relative hairline position). Any facial content that remains in the frame is incidental to the hair measurement and is never processed for identification.

**Use, sharing, and storage.** A captured photo — including any incidental facial content — is used for one purpose only: to produce hair-measurement scores for you, and, if you choose to use the optional before/after composite feature, to produce that composite image. Photos are stored only in the Application's private storage on your own device. The Service Provider operates no image server, no cloud photo backup, and no account system, and never receives, stores, or has access to a copy of your photos. Photos are never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train any model by the Service Provider, and never shared with anyone other than the AI processor described below.

**Third-party processing.** At the moment you request a measurement, the cropped photo is transmitted over an encrypted connection to Google LLC's Gemini AI models via Firebase AI Logic, solely so the model can return the numeric hair scores (or the composite image). Google acts as a processor for this request and its handling is governed by the [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) terms. No other third party — including advertising, analytics, or attribution partners — receives your photos. Your photos are not attached to analytics or advertising events.

**Retention and deletion.** The Service Provider retains no copy of any photo: transmission to the AI processor is transient, lasting only as long as the request needs, and no image is persisted on any server controlled by the Service Provider. On your device, photos and their measurements remain until you delete them: you can delete an individual measurement, or delete all photos and measurement history at once, in Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the Application from your device also removes all captured photos. You may also export your data from Settings, or contact the Service Provider at asiyegy@icloud.com with any deletion request.

## Health-Related Information

Photos of your scalp and the measurements derived from them may be considered health-related information. They are processed solely to provide the service, are never sold, and are not used for advertising. Densio is not a medical device: its scores are for personal documentation only and are not a diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice.
